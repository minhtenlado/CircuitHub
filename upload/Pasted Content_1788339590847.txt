Stock = 1

Two buyers attempt to purchase simultaneously.

Only one transaction may successfully reserve the final item.

* database transactions
* atomic updates
* row-level locking
* optimistic/pessimistic concurrency strategy

# 135. SECURITY BUSINESS RULES

2. Backend recalculates all financial values.
3. Inventory must be validated atomically.
4. Sellers cannot sell before approval.
5. Buyer reviews require valid purchase.
6. Withdrawal cannot exceed available balance.
7. Refund cannot exceed amount paid.
8. Payment callbacks must be verified.
9. Webhooks must be idempotent.
10. Seller ownership must be verified.
11. Buyer ownership must be verified.
12. Admin actions must be audited.
13. Private digital assets must not be public.
14. Uploaded files must be validated and scanned.
15. Sensitive data must not be exposed through APIs.
